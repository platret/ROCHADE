# Design notes

How the ROCHADE screens were designed. Before each screen we searched Mobbin (iOS) for shipped apps that solve the same pattern, looked at 20 or more screens per search, kept 3 to 5 references from different apps and took only patterns, hierarchy and spacing, never a brand look.

## Principles that came out of the research

- One warm accent. Orange appears once per screen as a solid element (status icon, current step node, primary button) plus at most a 10 percent tint. Chevrons, links in lists and secondary icons stay grey.
- Facts in mono, sentences in Manrope. Only standalone dates, times and flat numbers are set in JetBrains Mono. Inside running sentences (reasons, banners) everything stays Manrope.
- Problems are explained, not painted red. A conflict is a badge with an icon, the exact reason in plain German, then the reassurance line. No full-screen red, no red wash.
- Three node states only on the journey rail: done, current, future. Exactly one node is current, which is where the single orange dot lives.
- Label over value. Caption label (13) above a larger value (mono 24 for the hero facts, 16 to 18 elsewhere). Older readers get body 16 everywhere and nothing below 13.
- Change is shown as old then new: old value grey and struck through or on the left, new value bold on the right, never coloured green or red.
- Lists are one grouped container with inset hairlines, rows 64 pt, no checkmarks, no radio buttons, the row itself is the action.
- Timelines use a fixed label column, a shared axis with month gridlines, flat fills and 1.5 pt outlines, never horizontal scrolling.
## References per screen

### Anmelden als (Rollen-Picker)

Pattern: Launch-time role/account picker as a single-column grouped list: small left-aligned brand header (mark + wordmark + one tagline line), a question heading ("Anmelden als") with one helper line, then tappable rows (neutral initials circle, household name, mono flat label + building, grey chevron) in one grouped container, and the administration role as a visually separated second group with a different leading icon. One tap selects, persists the choice and enters the app; no select-then-Continue step.
